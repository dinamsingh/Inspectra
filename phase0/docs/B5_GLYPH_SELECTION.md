# Pre-registered panel → measured-glyph assignment — blocker B5

**What B5 is:** *which* of the 400 printed glyph instances the camera actually measures,
fixed before any capture exists.

**Boundary with B3:** B3 settled **how** a glyph is located and deliberately mapped all
400 so that this selection could be made without the map encoding a preference
(`B3_GLYPH_ROI_MAP.md` §9). B5 chooses **which**.

**Changes nothing scientific:** no measurement algorithm, no `config/`, no P1-P7
threshold, no `k = 1.645`, no Solution Lock, no synthetic artefact, no B3/B4 machinery.
B2 and B6 untouched.

**Verdict: `B5 = CLOSED`** (§9).

---

| `compute_p4` | per-device errors after a global bias correction → every panel must be shot on both devices |

The design has 4 factors and only 20 cells: 5 heights × 4 shapes × 4 fonts × 20 panels.
Anything chosen here is a trade-off, so it is chosen by a **rule**, not panel by panel.

## 2. The assignment

`tools/make_glyph_selection.py` → `fixtures/physical/coupons/glyph_selection.json`
(**20 panels, `selection_hash 1a1b1dabea1421f2…`**), generated by a closed form rather
than hand-picked:

```
f = (i − 1) mod 4        # font group; panels.json already assigns fonts by i mod 4
p = (i − 1) div 4        # position in the font group (= sheet − 1)
c = (p + f) mod 5        # height class
nominal_h   = HEIGHTS[c]
glyph_shape = SHAPES[(f + c) mod 4]
```

Every balance claim below is **asserted by a test**, not stated in prose
(`tests/test_glyph_selection.py::TestBalanceInvariants`):

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| heights | 1.2 / 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.0 / 6.0 → **4 panels each** |
| shapes | BAR_I / H / T / RING_O → **5 panels each** |
| shape classes | FLAT_TOP 15, ROUND 5 — the shape proportion, both present |
| fonts | 4 fonts → **5 panels each** |
| height × shape | all **20 pairs distinct** → a complete 5 × 4 factorial, one panel per cell |
| font × height | all **20 pairs distinct** → font and height fully crossed |
| every font | sees all 4 shapes (one repeated, unavoidable with 5 panels) |
| every sheet | carries 4 **distinct** heights |
| P2's 3 mm class | **P03, P06, P09, P20** → 4 panels × 12 runs = **48 observations** |

One imbalance is left and named: shapes clump slightly *within* a print sheet (sheet 1 is
BAR_I, T, BAR_I, T). A sheet is a printing artefact, not an experimental factor — heights
are distinct within every sheet, and balancing shape-per-sheet as well would over-constrain
a 20-cell design.

## 3. Why balanced, and what it costs

The tempting alternative was to weight the 3 mm class, since **P2** is the headline
accuracy criterion. It was considered and rejected:

| Option | 3 mm observations | Other heights | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Balanced 4-4-4-4-4 (chosen)** | 48 | 48 each | complete factorial; height and shape effects estimable; encodes no guess about which height matters |
| Weighted 8-3-3-3-3 | 96 | 36 each | P2's SD estimated more precisely, but 1.2 mm — the hardest height — drops to 3 panels, the factorial breaks, and it presumes before any data exists that 3 mm is where the answer lies |

Two pieces of arithmetic behind that:

* **P2 is an SD, not a mean.** More observations sharpen the *estimate* of the SD, they do
  not shift its expected value. The relative standard error of an SD is about
  `1/√(2(n−1))`: ~10 % at n = 48, ~7 % at n = 96. On a 0.08 mm Go band, 10 % is ±0.008 mm.
  Adequate.
* **The real limitation is not the allocation.** Those 48 observations are 4 panels × 12
  runs, and runs within a panel share that panel's print, its mounting and its reference
  value. For the *between-panel* (print-to-print) component the effective sample is **4**,
  not 48 — a relative standard error near 41 %. That follows from the frozen design (20
  panels, one glyph each, A-14), not from how the 20 are allocated, and no allocation
  removes it. **Stated, not hidden:** P2 mixes within-panel and between-panel variance, and
  its between-panel part rests on four panels.

Choosing before any data exists is the decisive argument for balance: a selection cannot
be optimised against results that do not exist, so the honest optimisation target is
coverage, which is exactly what §1.6 asks for.

## 4. The P1 cross-check subset — the two open sub-decisions, closed

`P0_REFERENCE_PROCEDURE.md` §8 left two things to B5. Both are now decided:

1. **Must the cross-check subset include the 20 camera-measured glyphs?** **Yes — it *is*
   them.** P1's job is to bound the reference values that P2/P3/P4 actually consume. A
   subset drawn from elsewhere would bound the agreement of references nobody uses, which
   is the wrong quantity. Cross-checking any measured glyph's reference is therefore
   mandatory, and the validator raises `MEASURED_GLYPH_NOT_CROSS_CHECKED` if one is left
   out.
2. **How is it distributed across fonts, shapes and heights?** It inherits §2's balance by
   construction: all 5 heights, all 4 shapes, both classes, all 4 fonts.

And the arithmetic works out without a second decision: the criterion needs **≥ 15**, the
measured set is **20**, so the measured glyphs alone satisfy the minimum with margin. More
glyphs may be cross-checked — that is only a `WARN`
(`P1_SUBSET_BEYOND_MEASURED`) noting that the extras do not bound what P2/P3/P4 consume.

This resolves item **C9** in `B2_MINIMUM_SETUP.md` §7. It does **not** close B2: producing
the reference values still needs the scanner, the microscope and the certified standard.

## 5. Anti-tuning

The selection cannot be steered by a result, because it is a pure function of
`panels.json` and an index:

* `--check` re-derives it and fails on a single edited field
  (`test_cli_check_fails_on_a_tampered_selection`);
* `validate_selection.py` re-derives it independently and raises
  `SELECTION_NOT_DERIVED` on a hand edit;
* the manifest must carry the `selection_hash`; a missing or swapped one is an error, and
  **`run_real_batch.py` exits** rather than running;
* every nominal run must measure its panel's selected glyph —
  `RUN_MEASURES_UNSELECTED_GLYPH` — and the runner enforces that too;
* one panel may not contribute two glyphs: `PANEL_MEASURES_TWO_GLYPHS`;
* the allocation rule contains no term that could encode error, focus, contrast, ease or
  compliance — only panel index, font group and the frozen height/shape lists.

Stress runs are exempt by design: `P0_EXECUTION_PLAN.md` §1.4 gives them no accuracy
expectation and they feed **P6** only.

## 8. Tests

`tests/test_glyph_selection.py` — **49 tests**; suite total **310**. Covering: derivation
and determinism; every selected glyph resolves in the B3 map **and** is actually printed on
that panel; shape class agrees with `p0.render.glyph_shape_class`; all nine balance
invariants; the P1 subset's identity, minimum, span and the four ways to break it; coverage
enforcement (missing height, single shape class, empty P2 class, duplicate panel, empty
selection, hand edit, inconsistent key, stale map hash); manifest enforcement (wrong glyph,
wrong height, two glyphs on one panel, unselected panel, missing and swapped
`selection_hash`, run without a panel, stress exemption, panels not yet shot); reference
cross-check; CLI exit codes.
